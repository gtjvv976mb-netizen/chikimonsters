Privacy

Chikoria, chikimonsters.com, and the Chikoria iOS app.

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This page is written to be accurate about what the software actually does, which is the hard part. It is not legal advice and it has not been checked against GDPR, CCPA or the App Store's own requirements. Have someone qualified read it before the app is submitted, and change whatever they tell you to.

The short version

Your Chikoria account is a Solana wallet address. We hold what you did in the game against that address — your chikimon, your satchel, your quests, your battles. We do not hold your name, your email, your phone number or your location, because we never ask for them.

What we hold

  • Your wallet address. It is your account name. It is public on the Solana blockchain by design, and it is how your saved game is found.
  • Your game progress. Creatures, items, levels, quest state, battle results — the cloud save the website and the app both read.
  • A device link. When you pair the iOS app, the server issues that device a token so it can play your account without a wallet. It is stored in the iPhone's Keychain and sent to our server to sign in. It authorises playing; it cannot sign a transaction, spend, sell or move anything.
  • A device identifier we generate. A random value made on your device the first time the app runs, so you can see and revoke that device from the website. It is not your phone's identifier, and it is not shared with anyone.
  • Ordinary server logs. Requests to our servers are logged, including IP address, for security and debugging.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, receive or store your private key or seed phrase. Ever. Nobody legitimate ever will.
  • The iOS app contains no wallet and cannot construct, sign or send a transaction.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites. The iOS app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

The iOS app specifically

The app is the game's world with the money taken out of it. It has no wallet, no purchases and no trading. It talks to two places and nothing else: this website, and the Chikoria game server. It is not permitted to reach anything else — not a blockchain node, not a marketplace, not an advertiser — and that restriction is enforced in the app itself, not merely promised here.

If you are curious, the enforcement is public: chiki-ios.js is the file that does it.

The blockchain part

Some things you earn are recorded on the Solana blockchain. That ledger is public and permanent, and it is not ours to edit or erase. Anything written there stays there, whatever we do with our own records. That is the nature of the technology, not a policy choice.

Your choices

  • Unlink a device. On the website, open Realm Link and revoke it. The token stops working immediately. You can also unlink from inside the app.
  • Delete your account. Ask from the app, under Account, or write to the address below. We delete your Chikoria progress from our servers. Assets held on-chain belong to your wallet and are outside our reach.
  • Ask what we hold. Write to the address below with your wallet address.

Children

Chikoria is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold data about them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the game's news panel rather than quietly editing it.
