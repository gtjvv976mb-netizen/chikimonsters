Chikoria support

Answers to the things that go wrong, and how to reach a person.

Pairing the app

Where do I get a code?

On a computer or tablet, open chikimonsters.com/link, sign in with your wallet, and press New code. Type that code into the app.

I only have a phone

The code has to come from the website, because that is where your wallet is. You can open chikimonsters.com/link in your phone's browser too — you just need your wallet app on the same phone to sign in there.

The code says it is not valid

  • Codes last about ten minutes. Press New code and try again.
  • Each code works once. If you already used it, mint another.
  • Check for a typo — the code is letters and numbers only, and case does not matter.

The app says my link ended

That happens when the device was unlinked from the website, or the link expired. Pair again with a new code. Nothing on your account is lost.

Your account and your things

Where did my items go?

The app and the website share one account, so anything you gather in the app is on the website and the other way round. If something is missing, check you are signed in to the same wallet in both places — the app's Account screen shows which one it is using.

Can I sell or trade in the app?

No. The app is for playing. Selling and trading are not part of it.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. Pair each one with its own code, then switch between them on the Account screen.

How do I unlink a device?

In the app, Account → Unlink this iPhone. Or from the website's Realm Link page, where every linked device is listed with a Revoke button. Revoking takes effect immediately.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, Account → Delete my account. This removes your Chikoria progress from our servers and cannot be undone. Items held on the blockchain belong to your wallet and are not affected — we cannot delete those, and neither can anyone else.

The game itself

It is downloading for a long time

The world downloads once, the first time you play, and is then kept on your phone. On mobile data the app fetches a lighter version to save your plan. Wi-Fi is faster.

It crashed, or restarted while loading

That is usually the phone running out of memory. Close other apps and try again — the game will automatically use a lighter world for the next day. If it keeps happening, tell us which iPhone you have.

Can my device run it?

Open this check on the device. It answers in plain words and has a button to copy the result, which is exactly what we need if you contact us.

Contact us

Email: support@chikimonsters.com

We read everything and usually reply within two working days.

Please include: your wallet address (the one shown on the app's Account screen), what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. The Copy support details button under Account → Support gathers the version and device information for you.

You can also find us on Discord and X, though email reaches us most reliably.
