The game

Everything the island has, in your hand.

Chikoria on iPhone is the full game world — not a companion app. The same island, the same creatures and the same adventures, tuned for a phone.

Gather, craft, cook, fish

14 resources, 130 recipes and ten levels of tools. Fish the coasts for eight species and four sea legends. Everything is gathered and crafted by hand.

Clear the Wicked Temple

Take one creature into five escalating sanctums against the corrupted horde. Your deck is the creature's own ability cards. Clear all five and the vault opens.

Duel in the Chikiseum

Take a creature you raised into real-time duels against other trainers. The server runs every match — nothing is staked and nothing is wagered, it's just you and your team.

Hatch and raise a team

Twenty-one species across ten elemental lines and five ancient legendaries. Every creature levels to 50, learns up to twelve ability cards and grows a personality from how you play.

One account, everywhere

Play on your phone. Pick up on the web.

Everything you earn in the app — creatures, eggs, fish, resources, quest progress — is saved to your Chikoria account, and it's all there the next time you play on chikimonsters.com.

  1. New here? Just tap “Create an account”.No email, no password, no wallet. You're on the island in one tap.
  2. Already play on the web? Pair it.Sign in at chikimonsters.com/link, press “New code”, and type the code into the app. Your whole account comes with you.
  3. Keep it safe.An account made on your iPhone can be backed up to your chikimonsters.com account from the app, so you never lose it with the phone.

Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything?

No. Chikoria is free to play, and there are no purchases of any kind in the app.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Tap “Create an account” and play. The app has no wallet in it and never asks for one.

Is my progress shared with the web version?

Yes. The app plays your Chikoria account, so everything you gather, hatch and win is saved to it and waiting on chikimonsters.com — and the other way round.

Is there chat?

Not in the app. The app has no player-to-player chat or messages.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The game is a full 3D world, so a recent iPhone gives the smoothest ride. It plays in landscape.

How big is the download?

The app itself is small. The first time you open it, it downloads the game world — about 175 MB on mobile data, a little more on Wi-Fi — and keeps it, so later launches are quick.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete my account…. Details are in our privacy page.
