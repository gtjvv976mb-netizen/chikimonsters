Connect an app account

Made your account inside the Chikoria app and want to trade what you have found? In the app open Account → Connect a wallet, then type the code it shows here. Your island, your creatures and everything you have earned move onto this wallet.

This is one-way: the app account becomes this wallet. It only works on a wallet that has not played Chikoria before — connecting one that already has progress would mean merging two save files, and that always loses one of them.
