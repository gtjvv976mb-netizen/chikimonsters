🎮 How to Play

  • Sign in with Phantom — a free, gas-less message signature proves the wallet is yours (never a transaction; we never ask for private keys or seed phrases). Hold ≥500k $CHIKI and tap your egg to hatch (a hidden lucky number wins a Legendary!).
  • Move with WASD · Shift to run · Space to jump · right-drag to look · wheel to zoom. On phones: touch joystick + buttons.
  • Press E to gather trees, rocks, ores and more · craft tools at the Craft Shop to gather much faster.
  • Press P for your menu — quests, satchel, team, mounts, avatar and gear.
  • Battle corruptimons with your chikimon's ability cards (1–4 to play, R ends the turn, V auto) · duel trainers with G · enter the Chikoria Cup at the Chikiseum.
  • Follow the campaign — ACT I · MASTER OF CHIKORIA, 21 chapters across 3 parts, paying real $CHIKI from the reward pool.
  • Trade with players at the Trading Post — when your listing sells, 95% of the price lands in your purse automatically (5% burns as the market fee).
  • Top-left buttons — graphics tier, language (EN / 日 / 中) and music, remembered on your device.

Care for your team

Chikimons have HP, stamina, hunger and moods — feed them, let them rest, heal free at the Hospital, and bond with them to unlock their personalities.

You'll learn all of it in your first ten minutes — then everything you own, you'll earn out in the world.
